# App Store and Google Play audit

Requirement-by-requirement check of Plenty (web app plus the planned iOS and Android shells) against Apple's App Review Guidelines and Google Play's policies. Written 2026-10-03.

## How to read this, and what was and wasn't verified

**Status values:** **Met** (done and tested or visible in code), **Met by design** (true because of how the product is built), **Needs external action** (requires an account, credential, console setting or native build outside this repository), **Not verified** (couldn't confirm the requirement itself, or it needs a human decision), **Gap** (a real shortfall in the product that needs a change).

**Sources actually read:**

* **Apple App Review Guidelines**, `developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/`, fetched 2026-10-03. The text for 1.2, 1.4.1, 2.1, 2.3, 3.1.1, 3.1.2, 3.1.3, 4.0, 4.2, 4.8, 5.1.1, 5.1.2 and 5.1.4 below was returned by the page (the tool summarises pages, so wording is as returned, not byte-checked). The page shows no "last updated" date. **Section 5.6 (Developer Code of Conduct) was truncated out of every fetch (page and Apple's PDF) and is therefore NOT VERIFIED verbatim**; only a search-result summary was available.
* **Google Play, primary pages:** `support.google.com` and `play.google.com` are **blocked by the sandbox's egress proxy**, and `web.archive.org` is blocked too. The Policy Center text was therefore **not** read. What was read: `developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk` (page dated 2026-10-01) and `developer.android.com/google/play/billing/deprecation-faq`, and `developer.android.com/distribute/play-policies` (an index page). Everything else under Google Play comes from **search-result summaries of Google's help pages and third-party articles**, and is marked **NOT VERIFIED — re-check against the live policy before submission**.

Neither store's rules stand still (Apple's guideline 3.1.1 text and Google's deadlines both changed within the last year). Treat this as a checklist to re-run against the live text before each submission.

**State of the product assumed:** a Next.js web app that works today; native iOS and Android shells **do not exist in this repository** (`docs/billing.md`: "No native iOS/Android code exists here"), so everything a shell must do is listed as external action. Plans: Free, Plus ($4.99/month or $49.99/year), Family ($8.99/month or $89.99/year); Pro is defined but not offered; no free trial (`TRIAL_DAYS = 0`, `src/lib/billing/plans.ts`).

## Decisions and gaps that need a human first

| # | Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Analytics default.** Usage analytics are first-party, minimal and on by default with a per-person opt-out. Apple 5.1.1(ii) says "Apps that collect user or usage data must secure user consent for the collection, even if such data is considered to be anonymous". An opt-out may not satisfy a strict reading, particularly in the iOS shell. | Options: make analytics opt-in (at least on iOS), or show a first-run notice with a choice. Not changed here because it is a product decision. |
| 2 | **AI-generated recipes have no "report" button.** Google's AI-Generated Content policy (secondary sources) expects in-app reporting or flagging of offensive AI-generated content. | Add a "Report this recipe" action on recipes with `meals.source = 'ai'`, or confirm with Google's text that structured recipe suggestions are out of scope. |
| 3 | **Child accounts.** A child can have a restricted Child account that an adult invites. Sign-up has no age gate (it states "18 or over" and links Terms and Privacy), so a child could create the account, and `acceptInvitation` doesn't check who is joining. Apple 5.1.4 and children's privacy laws (COPPA, GDPR) apply to personal data collected from a minor. | Consider limiting under-18s to managed profiles (no email, no sign-in) or having the adult create the child account. Needs legal advice. |
| 4 | **Legal review** of the public Privacy Policy and Terms (`/privacy`, `/terms`): written from the code, not by a lawyer. Governing law and liability limits are generic and name no jurisdiction. | Required before launch. |
| 5 | **Operator identity.** `LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME`, `SUPPORT_EMAIL`, `PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL` must be set in production, otherwise the pages say the operator hasn't published contact details. Apple 1.2 asks for "Published contact information" and 5.6.2 for verifiable developer identity. | Set before submission. |
| 6 | **A "web-wrapper" risk (Apple 4.2).** If the iOS shell is only a WebView around the site, "Your app should include features, content, and UI that elevate it beyond a repackaged website." | Use native camera capture, the store purchase sheet and a native navigation shell; explain them in review notes. |

## What was changed in the product for this audit

* Receipt text, item lines and store labels are redacted before storage; photos have a retention policy with a scheduled sweep (`deleteExpiredReceiptImages`) and "Photo deleted" handling.
* Outside AI is consent-gated, plan-gated and fail-closed; the old unguarded `getProvider` is gone; the general preferences form can no longer set the AI answer; two fail-open defaults (`?? true` in receipts and meals) are removed.
* Account and household deletion cover every table (with a guard test), refuse to orphan a household, cancel web subscriptions first, and tell people about store subscriptions.
* Analytics: strict whitelist intake, rate limit, per-person opt-out, retention of 395 days, deletion with the household.
* Public `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/support`, `/delete-account`, `/account-deleted`; in-app Privacy & data centre.
* Housekeeping job removes expired sessions and spent reset tokens.

## Re-check before every submission

1. Re-read Apple 2.1, 2.3, 3.1.1–3.1.3, 4.2, 4.8, 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.1.4 and **5.6**, and Google's User Data, Data safety, Account deletion, Payments & Subscriptions, Families, AI-Generated Content and Permissions pages, from a network that can reach them.
2. Re-run the claims check in `docs/store/listing.md` against the exact build.
3. Open `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/support`, `/delete-account` in a signed-out browser with production env set: no placeholders, real contact details.
4. Walk account deletion end to end on a fresh account, including a household with a second person, and (in sandbox) one with an App Store or Play subscription.
